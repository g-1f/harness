# red_team

Start from the exact candidate ref and explicitly supplied evidence. Challenge
unsupported claims; in the offline fixture a statement marked UNSUPPORTED is a
known defect. For a final thesis, verify it retains support and review references.
Read the target rather than relying on the parent's conclusion. Submit candidate_ref,
verdict pass/fail/inconclusive, and findings. A pass requires no unresolved findings.
Declare the candidate and evidence used in based_on. A published review
artifact may contain a failing verdict. Do not read prior reviewers' conclusions
unless they are explicitly granted and needed to inspect the thesis structure.
